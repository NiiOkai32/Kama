Terms of service

Who can play

Kama is for adults aged 18 and older. All players must freely agree to take part. Anyone can skip a card, pause or leave at any time. An in-game ready check never replaces ongoing agreement.

Accounts and shared play

Use an email address you control and keep your password and Duet invitations private. In Duet, shared cards and prompts you deliberately reveal are visible to your partner. Share only material you have permission to share. Do not use Kama to harass anyone or access another person’s account.

See the privacy notice for details about data, storage and account deletion.

Pricing and refunds

Duet hosting is free during the Kama 2.0 test. The planned membership costs US$2.99 per month, with an estimated GHS price where available. Purchases and renewals are currently disabled. There are no Kama purchases through this app to cancel or refund.

If you see a charge you did not authorize, use your bank or payment provider’s private support channel. Keep payment and account details out of public GitHub issues.

Music and other services

Opening a playlist takes you to Spotify or Apple Music. Those services handle their own access, subscriptions, billing and refunds.

Project and support

Kama is published through the NiiOkai32/Kama project. Use its public issue tracker for general questions. For account deletion, sign in and open Game settings.
