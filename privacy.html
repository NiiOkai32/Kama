Your privacy. Your choice.

Updated 1 November 2026. Kama is for consenting adults aged 18 or older. Play is optional, and a ready check can be withdrawn at any time.

Your account

Supabase handles email, password authentication and sessions. Kama never stores passwords in its game database. Your session stays in this browser until you sign out or clear this device. GitHub Pages hosts the site; Supabase hosts account data in the EU. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and technical request logs.

What stays on this device

Custom cards, favourites, card images, custom forfeits, shuffle history, boundaries and game totals stay in this browser. They are shared by people using the same browser profile, even with different Kama accounts. Typed answers are held in the current page and cleared when a new card is drawn. Images are resized and re-encoded locally; they are not uploaded. Local storage is not an encrypted vault.

Usage tracking

Kama no longer sends account-linked gameplay analytics. Older opt-in events may still exist in the database; they contain an account ID, event name and time. Deleting your account removes those linked records. Turning tracking off does not erase hosting or security logs.

Remote Duet

Duet is a separate feature under preparation. It requires two signed-in accounts. The server holds room participants, shared cards, readiness, timers and prompts explicitly revealed to a partner. Typed answers are not sent. One host membership covers one invited partner. Checkout is disabled.

Invitations are bearer secrets. They can be pasted as a code or shared as a link with a URL fragment, which is not sent as part of an HTTP request. Copying a link still discloses its code to the recipient and possibly your clipboard or messaging service. Rooms lose access after 24 hours or membership expiry. Access expiry is not automatic deletion; closed or abandoned room records and backups can remain until retention cleanup. Leaving clears the active shared card and reveals.

Pause, hide and delete

Pause / Stop pauses local play and covers the table. Hide, or Escape when no dialog is open, replaces the page with a neutral screen. Quick hide does not erase browser history or stored data. Remote partners may still have content on their own devices.

In Game settings, Clear this device removes Kama's saved browser data and this project's local sign-in. It does not remove server account data or copies on other devices. Delete account requires your current password and a typed confirmation. It deletes your Supabase account, linked usage events, membership and hosted rooms, and clears this device. If you were a guest, the room is closed and your identity and revealed content are removed. Provider logs and backups follow provider retention. Exported decks and partner copies cannot be remotely erased.

Age and consent

The 18+ confirmation is a self-declaration, not verified age assurance. It is rechecked every 30 days on this browser. Only play with adults who freely agree. Theme boundaries are a convenience filter and may miss wording, especially in custom cards; discuss boundaries and skip whenever you want. Both players must confirm readiness before Fire or Mystery cards are revealed.

Backups and third parties

Custom-deck exports contain private text in a normal JSON file. Store or share them carefully. Kama uses a locally hosted copy of the Supabase client and system fonts, so opening the page does not contact a font or script CDN.

Questions and legal responsibilities

Use the repository's support page for general issues. Do not post passwords, email addresses, private prompts or invitation codes there. Applicable privacy obligations, including Ghana Data Protection Commission registration where required, need the operator's own assessment. This notice does not certify legal compliance.
